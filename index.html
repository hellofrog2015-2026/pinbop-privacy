Privacy Policy for PinBop

Last updated: November 2026

PinBop is an offline arcade game developed by an independent developer. Your privacy is our top priority, and the application is built to respect your personal data completely.

1. Information Collection and Use

PinBop does NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personal identifiable information (PII). We do not require account registration, and we do not track your activity across other apps or websites.

2. Game Data Storage

All your stage progression, high scores, and settings are saved locally on your device via standard iOS local storage. We do not operate external servers to host your gameplay data.

3. In-App Purchases (StoreKit)

In-app purchases (such as PRO Unlimited) are processed entirely and securely by Apple Media Services via the Apple StoreKit framework. We do not collect or have access to your payment information, credit cards, or billing address.

4. Apple Game Center

If you choose to use Apple Game Center for global leaderboards, your scores are submitted securely through official Apple Game Center APIs according to Apple's Privacy Policy.
